# SideSpark LinkedIn launch story design

## Purpose

Turn the existing 18-second SideSpark concept film into a personal hackathon recap for Tanveer Riaz's LinkedIn. The post should show the real sequence: Tanveer and Benny arrived without an idea, shaped SideSpark through conversation, built and presented a working ChatGPT Sites prototype in roughly 60–80 minutes, and entered public voting.

The outcome is not framed as a win. The value is the finished prototype, the people met, and the experience of building through conversation.

## Video

Create a concise landscape LinkedIn video by adding an opening and closing section around the existing 1280×720, 18-second H.264 film.

### Opening, approximately 5 seconds

- Use real photographs from the SideSpark blog: the hackathon room/build table and Tanveer with Benny.
- Copy:
  - `We arrived with no idea.`
  - `60–80 minutes later, SideSpark was live.`
- Keep the composition graphic and editorial: SideSpark indigo, coral, mint, yellow, bold rounded type, restrained movement, and no generic icons or AI-generated filler.

### Existing film, 18 seconds

- Preserve the current coffee, walking, and desk-side skill-swap film.
- Add a small persistent SideSpark mark only if it remains readable without covering people or key action.
- Do not imply that the fictional people in the film attended the event.

### Closing, approximately 6 seconds

- Use the real Tanveer-and-Benny photograph, then the hackathon group photograph.
- Copy:
  - `Built with GPT-5.6 in Codex + ChatGPT Sites`
  - `Maybe we win. Either way, we built it.`
  - `Read the story · tanveerriaz.me`
- End on the SideSpark wordmark and a short, readable URL. Full clickable URLs belong in the LinkedIn post or first comment, not as dense on-screen text.

Target duration: 29–31 seconds. Target format: 1280×720 H.264 with AAC or silent audio accepted by LinkedIn. Captions must remain within mobile-safe margins and each text card must stay readable long enough to scan.

## LinkedIn post

Match Tanveer's established pattern:

- Open with a concrete scene or admission.
- Use specific numbers and visible actions instead of launch language.
- Explain SideSpark in one short paragraph.
- Describe building through conversation on a phone and laptop.
- Say clearly that public voting is open and winning is uncertain.
- Treat the people met and the experience as a worthwhile result already.
- Thank and tag Benny Tan, Gabriel Chua, Sherry Jiang, and OpenAI.
- Keep the Astra sentence curious rather than predictive. GPT-6 Astra is rolling out, but it is not presented as part of this build.
- Put the blog, live SideSpark site, and gallery links in the first comment so the post itself stays readable.
- Use a small set of relevant hashtags rather than a long promotional block.

## Truth and safety

- The 60–80 minute claim is Tanveer and Benny's experience, not a promise that every site takes that long.
- The site was built with GPT-5.6 in Codex and ChatGPT Sites; Astra is mentioned only as the next capability Tanveer is curious about.
- SideSpark remains a hackathon prototype. Its in-product profiles and film characters are fictional.
- Do not claim that SideSpark won. Public voting is still open when the post is prepared.
- The post may publicly tag people and organizations only after Tanveer confirms the final copy and upload.
